    Workshop guide

    Use the webcam

    • Point, then make a fist to grab. Move your hand and open it to place the organ.
    • Make an L with your thumb and index finger. Widen or narrow the gap to zoom.
    • Pinch empty space and move to rotate. With Pan on, pinch and move to shift the view.
    • Hold up two fingers to toggle labels or switch X-ray layers.
    • Pinch with both hands and move them apart to zoom.

    Use mouse, touch or keyboard

    • Drag an organ to move it and drag empty space to rotate. Use Pan to shift the entire view.
    • Use the on-screen zoom controls or scroll.
    • Use Tab and Enter to choose an organ, label, or placement target.
    • Use L to toggle labels and R to reset the model.
    Set up Body Scan

    Choose Body Scan and enable the camera. Select Upper body to track shoulders and arms while seated, or Full body to track legs and feet as well. The preview, cursor, and model follow the same mirror view.

    The preview shows green lines and dots on tracked hands and blue lines on tracked body joints. Video stays on this device.

    Aim the cursor at an organ and make a fist. Bring your hand closer or farther to adjust depth, then open it to place. Select Return to body to put the organ back.

    PC Assembly controls

    Drag equipment directly from the table. You can also click to pick it up, change views, and click to release. To use a keyboard, choose an item from the list, move it with the arrow keys, and press Enter to drop or Escape to set it back.

    Release a piece over a clear spot on the worktop to leave it there. Near the edges, the worktop supports the entire piece.

    Three-hour workshop plan
    20 min
    Introduce computer vision, 3D and spatial AR.
    25 min
    Demonstrate a heart extraction and return.
    40 min
    Watch the hand landmarks and test how pinching changes their distances.
    50 min
    In teams, edit an organ fact, layer instruction, or challenge timing in the source.
    30 min
    Rotate through heart exploration, reconstruction, Anatomy X-Ray, and Body Scan.
    15 min
    Present what changed and what each team observed.
